RBLine 4 · Technique

Reverse Brainstorming

How could this feel cheap, scary or boring to a 12-year-old? Then invert.

■ SERVICE NOTICE  Ten ways to break the ride were reported. Each one has a points switch that sends it the other way.
  1. FailRB1

    A wall of grey filters before any school.

    Invert

    Show schools first, tools tucked away (done on phone; extend with a “best next step” card).

  2. FailRB2

    Red “Stricter” everywhere reads as failing.

    Invert

    Frame stricter schools as “stretch” choices with a calm colour and the line strip, keep the honest label.

  3. FailRB3

    Generic stock icons.

    Invert

    A small custom line-icon set drawn on the same 20px grid for interests and steps.

  4. FailRB4

    Every page looks the same.

    Invert

    Each step gets its own treatment: score = big numerals, group = ticket choice, home = map, interests = chip board, list = browsing, six = line.

  5. FailRB5

    Silent waiting.

    Invert

    Every wait has a moving line and words saying what is happening.

  6. FailRB6

    The app reacts only after the student acts.

    Invert

    Suggest the next action before they look for it (one step ahead card on every main screen).

  7. FailRB7

    The end has no payoff.

    Invert

    My six as the climax, a line diagram and a shareable ticket.

  8. FailRB8

    Jargon labels.

    Invert

    Plain words and a “why” line.

  9. FailRB9

    Flat white page with no warmth.

    Invert

    A soft tinted ground band behind step heroes, and the brand ring mark used as the progress station.

  10. FailRB10

    Motion that distracts.

    Invert

    Motion only on load, on a click, or for the dealt moments; reduced motion rests everything.

Fifty-eight stations later, three insights joined the lines into one route, and the session decided what to build.

Insight 1

Three ideas are one idea in three hats.

The stepper station line, the school card line strip and the My six line diagram are one MRT-line visual language running through the whole flow. It is local, readable by a 12-year-old, and it doubles as the travel theme.

Stepper station line School card line strip My six line diagram One MRT-line visual language
Insight 2

One step ahead is a single “next step” component.

It reads the state and offers the one best action. Tap a state to see the component read it.
